## Why alternatives rank lower

Reproductive Potential and Brain Health explicitly need meaningful change over time and population appropriate validation. A four week questionnaire demo would underdeliver those outcomes. Genetic counselling adds familial disclosure and high consequence probabilistic interpretation. The two worker health statements require realistic signals and field or historical validation; fabricated sensors and maps are weak evidence. Mental wellbeing requires credible human crisis pathways the team does not currently have.

Healthy Behaviors is feasible but evidence of sustained change cannot mature in this window and the workflow is crowded. Screening-to-Care is the strongest alternative: clear commercial value and excellent demo potential. However, a simulated tracker without real provider ownership and closure evidence risks demonstrating record keeping rather than actual closed loop care.

Male Reproductive Health can demonstrate an entire private preparation journey with clinically bounded educational content and fictional inputs. It still needs professional review and cannot claim improved fertility or referrals from a synthetic test suite. Its defensible angle is the coherent workflow plus documented safety and comprehension, not a novel AI model.

## Sensitivity and reversal conditions

Male now scores 77 versus Screening-to-Care at 70. If its solo feasibility falls from 4 to 2 and evidence opportunity from 3 to 2, it falls to 67, below Screening-to-Care at 70. An actual clinic partner with referral workflow access could also raise Screening-to-Care's feasibility and evidence scores. Therefore the recommendation depends on a tightly bounded scope, not innate superiority of the topic.

Revisit with the team if organizers reject simulated pathways, a clinical reviewer cannot review even draft education, or a clinic offers an immediately usable alternative workflow. Do not silently change challenge. If review is unavailable, first reduce intended use to an explicitly unvalidated simulation and disclose this competitive weakness.
